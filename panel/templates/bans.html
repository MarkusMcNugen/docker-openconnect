Each sign-in to the VPN and this panel is scored from 0 to 100 against where and how the account signed in before: a place far from every usual one, a move faster than travel, a session open far away, and a network, country, device or hour of the day it has not used. From 50 the sign-in goes through with an alert; from 80 it is refused, with an alert, once the account has 30 days and 10 sign-ins behind it and is not on vacation. A check that cannot run lets the sign-in through.
