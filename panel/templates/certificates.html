The VPN server's key pin is what clients check; it changes only when the server's key does. The image's CA issues the server's and the panel's certificates unless SERVER_CERT or PANEL_CERT names your own. Trust the CA's file in your browser to sign in to the panel without a warning. VPN users' certificates come from a CA of their own and sign in only when Sign-in on the Settings page allows certificates. Renew issues a new certificate the same way and keeps the old one valid until it expires; Revoke stops one at once and ends its user's sessions. Times are in your browser's time zone.
